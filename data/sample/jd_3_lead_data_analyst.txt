Role: Lead Data Analyst - Product & Growth
Company: ShopStream Commerce
Location: Chicago, IL (Hybrid)
Job Type: Full-time

About ShopStream:
ShopStream is a fast-growing live video shopping marketplace. We are looking for a Lead Data Analyst to lead our product analytics, experimentation framework, and data visualization initiatives.

Responsibilities:
- Own the product experimentation and A/B testing framework, advising engineering and product managers on sample size sizing, hypothesis formulation, and variance reduction.
- Build clean, reproducible data models in Snowflake using dbt to support self-serve analytics across the business.
- Design world-class executive dashboards and exploratory reports in Tableau and Looker.
- Analyze retention, churn, and viral acquisition loops using cohort modeling and survival analysis in SQL and Python.
- Mentor junior and mid-level data analysts on SQL best practices, data storytelling, and dimensional modeling.

Requirements:
- 5+ years of data analytics or business intelligence experience in tech, e-commerce, or high-growth environments.
- Advanced proficiency in SQL (window functions, query plan optimization, CTEs) on modern data warehouses (Snowflake, BigQuery, or Redshift).
- Expert-level data visualization skills in Tableau, Looker, or Power BI.
- Hands-on experience with dbt (data build tool) and semantic layer definitions.
- Solid understanding of statistical testing (A/B testing, hypothesis tests, sample size determination).
- Strong communicator capable of synthesizing complex analytical insights for C-level executives.

Nice to Have:
- Proficiency in Python or R for advanced data wrangling and statistical modeling.
- Experience with product event analytics platforms (Amplitude, Mixpanel, Segment).
- Prior experience managing or mentoring data analysts.
