Role: Staff Site Reliability Engineer (SRE / Cloud Platform)
Company: Nexus Enterprise Cloud
Location: Seattle, WA (or Remote)
Job Type: Full-time

About Nexus Enterprise Cloud:
Nexus builds mission-critical infrastructure software powering modern enterprise SaaS applications. We are seeking an experienced SRE to scale our global multi-region cloud platform, enhance reliability, and lead incident response automation.

Responsibilities:
- Build and operate large-scale Kubernetes clusters running on AWS and hybrid cloud environments.
- Define and implement Infrastructure as Code using Terraform and GitOps practices (ArgoCD or Flux).
- Establish Service Level Objectives (SLOs), Service Level Indicators (SLIs), and error budgets across all engineering teams.
- Enhance observability stacks utilizing Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry, and distributed tracing.
- Participate in on-call rotation, conduct blameless post-mortems, and eliminate operational toil through automated tooling (Go or Python).

Minimum Requirements:
- 5+ years experience in SRE, DevOps, or Cloud Infrastructure Engineering.
- Production expertise managing Kubernetes clusters (EKS/GKE) and Helm charts.
- Deep knowledge of AWS services (networking, VPC peering, IAM, compute, security).
- Strong proficiency in Terraform for automated infrastructure provisioning.
- Practical experience with observability tooling (Prometheus, Grafana, Datadog).
- Working proficiency in Go, Python, or Bash for systems automation.

Preferred:
- CKA (Certified Kubernetes Administrator) certification.
- Hands-on experience with service mesh technologies (Istio, Linkerd) and zero-trust networking.
- Experience managing SOC2 and ISO27001 compliance automation.
